Islamabad, The Beautiful

Islamabad is the capital city of Pakistan, nestled against the scenic backdrop of the Margalla Hills at the northern edge of the Pothowar Plateau. Built in the 1960s as a carefully planned modern city to replace Karachi, it was officially inaugurated in 1967.
Known for its wide tree-lined avenues, peaceful environment, and high standard of living, the city is home to iconic landmarks such as the tent-inspired Faisal Mosque, the Pakistan Monument, and extensive green spaces like Fatima Jinnah Park and the Margalla Hills National Park.
